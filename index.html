Health tips

Small habits that help

Sitting a lot?

Stand up and walk for a minute every hour. Keep your feet flat and your back supported.

Stiff neck

Slowly tilt your head toward each shoulder and hold for a few seconds. Never force it or bounce.

Sleep better

Keep the same bedtime, dim the lights and put your phone away. A warm shower can help your body relax.

These tips are general advice, not a replacement for an examination. If pain is severe or does not go away, please come and see us.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does a session take?

It depends on the treatment. We’ll tell you the time when you book.

Does Hijama (wet cupping) hurt?

Most people feel a tight pulling sensation rather than sharp pain. Our therapist explains each step and stops if you are uncomfortable.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes that are easy to move in. We will cover you with a clean sheet during treatment.

Do you come to my home, and does it cost extra?

Yes, we do home visits around Entebbe. Ask us when you book and we will give you the cost for your area.

Do I need to book first?

Booking is best so there is no waiting, but call or WhatsApp us and we will do our best to fit you in.
